# Third-party icon geometry

Technology brand silhouettes in this icon theme are derived from
[Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) 16.29.0, released under
[CC0-1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).

The classic Java cup silhouette is derived from
[Devicon](https://devicon.dev/) 2.17.0, released under the
[MIT License](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/blob/master/LICENSE).

All source marks were normalized to the theme's single `#1035DE` color.
Product names and logos may remain trademarks of their respective owners.
